# Privacy Policy — MergeImages

MergeImages processes files and messages voluntarily sent by users to perform requested operations. Processed images, PDFs, GIFs, OCR text and QR contents are not automatically sent to the administrator.

The service may process Telegram ID, public name, language, settings, usage events and support tickets. General activity logs should not store usernames; the legacy database column may remain for compatibility. Usernames are used only in the support-ticket workflow when needed to contact the user.

Aggregated metrics may be shared with the administrator, including counts of actions, generated images, QR codes, OCR operations, conversions and active users. When a user opens a support ticket, the ticket text, public name, username and Telegram ID may be sent to the administrator so the issue can be investigated and answered. Users should not include passwords, tokens or private documents in tickets.

Visual search requires explicit confirmation before an image is uploaded to Telegra.ph or Catbox.moe and then used by Google Lens. These services have their own retention policies. Mini App endpoints use Telegram-signed `initData`; CORS is restricted through `MINIAPP_ALLOWED_ORIGINS`, and CORS is not authentication. The diagnostics endpoint is administrator-only.

The support message may be sent at most once after seven days of use and, separately, once when the credit quota is reached. The operator must define retention periods for temporary files, database records, logs and tickets, and provide deletion or anonymization procedures. Never publish tokens, API keys, production databases or credentials.
